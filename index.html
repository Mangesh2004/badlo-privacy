Badlo PDF Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy describes how the Badlo PDF Android app handles data. You can use Badlo without creating an account.

Documents, scans and photos

Badlo opens PDFs, photos and folders you select, and accepts documents you send to it from other apps. It processes your files, edits, scans and recognized text on your device. Badlo uses a PDF password to unlock the selected document and does not save the password. When you pick a file stored in the cloud, your chosen file provider handles access to its service.

When you export or share a file, you choose the destination or receiving app. That service handles the copy according to its own privacy policy. Badlo does not operate a server that receives your documents.

Google ML Kit and diagnostics

Badlo uses Google ML Kit for text recognition and offers Google’s document scanner. Google processes the scans, images and recognized text on your device. Google Play services may download scanner components. Google ML Kit sends technical information to Google over HTTPS, including device and app information, identifiers associated with the device or app installation, which ML Kit features are used, and details such as performance metrics and error codes. Google uses these metrics to maintain, diagnose and improve its services and detect misuse. See ML Kit’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Badlo does not display ads or sell personal data.

Storage, retention and deletion

Badlo can retain recent document shortcuts, imported copies, working files, edit history, drafts and preferences in app storage on your device. Text drafts are protected using Android’s Keystore. Remove a recent entry to delete its shortcut and cached import. Clear Badlo’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its private data. Copies saved to other locations remain there until you delete them. Google handles its ML Kit metrics under its own privacy policy.
